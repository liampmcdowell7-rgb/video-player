# Platform audit (tvOS)

Status: **PROVISIONAL**. Research and writing only. Nothing here is compiled, run on an Apple TV, or registered with Apple.
Pages were read on 2026-11-17 (see "Sources read"). Anything not read from an Apple page is marked **UNVERIFIED**.
Decision words in the table: **build**, **already built** (logic exists and is tested in WSL, never compiled for tvOS), **not possible on tvOS**, **cut by the user**, **needs the user's decision** (a recommendation and the alternatives are given in the section after the table).

## What the loopback-served media provides (the contract the system player sees)

Read from `Packages/VideoPlayerCore/Sources/RemuxEngine/Playlist.swift` and `docs/spikes/REMUX.md`:
- One HLS master playlist (`#EXT-X-VERSION:7`, fMP4 with `EXT-X-MAP`, VOD, `#EXT-X-ENDLIST`), `VIDEO-RANGE` (SDR/PQ/HLG), `SUPPLEMENTAL-CODECS` for Dolby Vision, `FRAME-RATE`, `RESOLUTION`, and one `EXT-X-MEDIA TYPE=AUDIO` rendition per audio track (language, channels, default).
- **No subtitle renditions today.** Playlist.swift states "Subtitles are not part of the playlists: the app draws them itself". This is the root of the subtitle conflict (row 2).
- Served over `http://127.0.0.1:<ephemeral port>`; `project.yml` has `NSAllowsLocalNetworking: true` and no other ATS exception.
- No chapters, no title or artwork metadata in the playlist. Title and artwork are supplied from the app side (`externalMetadata`, row 1).

### Cut list, stays cut
Profiles, parental controls, Trakt, Ambient Mode, AI upscaling, Surprise Me, local ratings, Rewatch list, Wake-on-LAN, SharePlay, scrub thumbnails, dual subtitles, social features (docs/FEATURE_BACKLOG.md). Where a cut item is also part of a system feature:
- **System multi-user (row 12)**: the user cut in-app *profiles*; the system's per-user data separation is a different mechanism and is therefore a separate decision (D2), not a reversal.
- **SharePlay**: the Apple TV system offers it; cut, nothing is built, and no `GroupActivities` capability is declared.
- **Parental controls**: cut in-app; the system's Screen Time / content restrictions exist and are not hooked up. The age-rating answer "Parental Controls: none" is therefore accurate for our own UI (see APPSTORE_READINESS).
- **Scrub thumbnails**: cut; the system player shows its own scrubber. HIG mentions thumbnail tracks only for visionOS ([Playing video](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-video)), so nothing is lost on tvOS (the page does not say whether tvOS shows thumbnails; **UNVERIFIED**).
- **Dual subtitles**: cut; one subtitle source at a time (row 2).

## Other findings (row 15)
- **Resume UX rule**: the HIG says "avoid asking people if they want to resume playback" and "show the expected content immediately", including "immediately presenting your own black screen" when launched from the TV app ([Playing video](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-video)). This app has no TV-app launch path (row 13), but the same rule applies to the Top Shelf Play button.
- **Overlays above video**: "avoid large, distracting overlays"; "some devices are prone to image retention" so keep Playback Stats and PGS bitmaps translucent and short-lived; interactive overlays need a 0.5 s minimum delay to pause playback ([Playing video](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-video)).
- **Swipe-up is reserved** by `AVPlayerViewController` (WWDC20 10176); any custom panel (our File Details, track lists) must use `customOverlayViewController` or `customInfoViewControllers`, never a swipe-up recogniser.
- **Info tab actions**: `infoViewActions` holds at most two actions ([page](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avkit/customizing-the-tvos-playback-experience)); the first is "Play from beginning" by default.
- **Content tabs**: `customInfoViewControllers` for File Details and Playback Stats is the HIG-conforming place; the Info tab shows only title/subtitle in the title view, the rest in the Info tab.
- **TLS**: tvOS 26 sets minimum TLS 1.2 for URLSession/Network for apps linked on or after OS 26; tvOS 27 tightens TLS "for select system processes" ([tvOS 26 notes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/tvos-release-notes/tvos-26-release-notes), [tvOS 27 notes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/tvos-release-notes/tvos-27-release-notes)). Impact on WebDAV/Jellyfin servers with old TLS: the app must say "server too old to connect securely" (device test).
- **Speech framework is not available on tvOS** (tvOS 26 notes): no on-device speech features.
- **Background Assets replaces On Demand Resources** (tvOS 27 notes): the app uses no ODR, nothing to change.
- **AsyncImage** gets HTTP caching in tvOS 27: poster images should still go through our own cache (STORAGE_MODEL, caches purgeable).
- **A subtitles-on-mute issue** exists in the tvOS 27 release notes (subtitles-on-mute logic); device test.
